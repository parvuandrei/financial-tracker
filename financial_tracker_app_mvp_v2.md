# Financial Tracker App — MVP

## Purpose
A personal finance dashboard that helps users understand **where they are**, **where their money goes**, and **where they are heading**.

## Core Features
- **Transaction tracking** — income, expenses, investments, transfers, one-offs
- **True monthly burn** — excludes investments, transfers, vacations, and exceptional expenses
- **Net worth tracking** — cash, investments, pensions, real estate, cars, debt
- **Balance projection** — month-by-month future cash balance
- **Scenario planning** — salary changes, career breaks, vacations, house purchase, bonuses, investment plans
- **Investment tracking** — portfolio value, monthly contributions, growth assumptions
- **Goals & milestones** — e.g. 100k invested, 1M net worth, house fund
- **Runway calculation** — how long liquid assets cover normal spending
- **Insights** — simple observations based on spending, liquidity, asset allocation, and projections
- **Multi-currency** — RON, EUR, USD
- **Import / export** — Excel/CSV in and out
- **Preference wizard** — setup currency, monthly baseline, projection horizon, and custom tracking rules

## Example Preference Rule
A user may choose to ignore small daily cash spending and model it as a fixed daily deduction instead.

Example:
- Ignore individual expenses below **100 RON**
- Deduct **100 RON/day** automatically from the projected balance
- Keep these small transactions out of detailed expense tracking

This keeps the app useful without forcing users to log every coffee, snack, or small cash purchase.

## MVP Principle
**Manual-first, simple, private, configurable, exportable.**

The differentiator is not expense tracking alone. It is combining:

> **cashflow + net worth + future balance + life-event scenarios**

into one clear financial cockpit.
