Quick setup

1. Choose your base currency

This is the currency for your saved baseline budget. Sample transactions and scenarios keep their stated currencies.

2. Calculate your net worth

Add the current value of what you own and the balances you owe. Leave unused items at zero or remove them.

Use one currency for every amount. Changing this selection does not convert values. Enter full property and vehicle values as assets; enter outstanding loans as liabilities.

3. Small daily spending rule

If you do not want to log coffee, snacks, small cash purchases, or other minor expenses, set a daily amount to deduct automatically.

Example: with 100 RON/day, the app deducts roughly 3,000 RON/month from projected cashflow without requiring individual transactions.
